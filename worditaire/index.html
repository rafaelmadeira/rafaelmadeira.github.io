Prototype rules: minimum 4 letters. Letter values use standard English Scrabble values. Every letter beyond 4 adds +5 points. Empty slots are allowed only before or after the word, not inside it. Hidden tableau cards stay hidden while a letter is sitting tentatively in the word row. Turned cards remain face-up in a pile: when you take the top one, the previous turned card becomes visible and available. Moving a turned card into the word row still locks drawing a new unseen card until those moved turned cards are scored or parked in an empty tableau column. Moving a visible letter into an empty tableau column is a committed move, and scoring a valid word also commits the current position, so Undo starts fresh after either kind of committed move. Word checking now uses a much larger local English dictionary (84,598 playable 4–10 letter words), loaded from a companion file, so no API or internet lookup is needed during play.
