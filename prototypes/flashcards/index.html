HOW IT WORKS

Study first. Score second.

01

Read the prompt, recall the answer, then reveal the card.

02

Choose Not good if you could not recall it, or Good if you remembered.

03

Earn XP on successful recalls. A separate scheduler decides when each card returns.

XP: Good = 100 base points; Not good = 0. Each previous miss adds 15% (up to +60%). A successful retry in the same session earns 25% of normal XP. XP never changes the review schedule.

Spaced repetition: Not good = repeat later this session, due again in 10 minutes. Good = 1 day after the first success, then 3 days, then about 2.5× the previous interval. A miss resets the success streak. Extra practice changes neither reviews nor XP. This uses a simplified SM-2-inspired schedule, not FSRS.
