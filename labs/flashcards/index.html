HOW IT WORKS

Study first. Score second.

01

Read the prompt, recall the answer, then reveal the card.

02

Rate your recall as No recall, Incorrect, Correct or Effortless.

03

Score each attempt, recover from setbacks and earn one-time mastery bonuses. Review dates remain independent.

XP: the first correct recall earns 100 base XP; subsequent correct recalls earn progressively less (minimum 20). Each unresolved miss adds +25 comeback XP to the next success, but the miss itself costs 40 XP. No recall and Incorrect have identical penalties. Reaching a 30-day review interval for the first time earns a one-off +500 mastery bonus. XP does not affect review timing.

Spaced repetition: No recall and Incorrect both repeat soon and become due in about 10 minutes. Correct uses 1 day, then 3 days, then approximately 2.5×; Effortless uses 3 days, then 7 days, then approximately 2.8×. A failed recall resets the successful streak. Extra practice never changes XP or scheduling. This is an experimental simplified scheduler, not Anki FSRS. A 30-day interval is an arbitrary game milestone, not permanent learning.
