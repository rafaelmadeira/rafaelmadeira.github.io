READING YOUR GARDEN

Every memory has a season.

01

Each square is a card. Gray means never opened; green indicates estimated recall health.

02

Hover or focus on a square for its age, growth stage, health, stability and next review.

03

Click a square for one card, or Study deck for a session. Health fades with time and recovers after recall.

XP: the first correct recall earns 100 base XP; subsequent correct recalls earn progressively less (minimum 20). Each unresolved miss adds +25 comeback XP to the next success, but the miss itself costs 40 XP. No recall and Incorrect have identical penalties. Reaching a 30-day review interval for the first time earns a one-off +500 mastery bonus. XP does not affect review timing.

Review: No recall and Incorrect both repeat soon. Correct and Effortless progressively increase intervals. The existing schedule is preserved; it is simplified and is not Anki FSRS.

Health: a provisional forgetting-curve estimate based on the scheduled review interval as a stability proxy, not a calibrated prediction. Exposures and failed recalls use conservative display-only starting values. A square never turns gray again once opened. Growth stage reflects the longest interval reached; a lapse does not erase the history.

Early study: individual cards can be reviewed before they are due. Those ratings update the study record but have no XP reward or penalty. Full-deck extra practice remains optional and does not change the schedule.
