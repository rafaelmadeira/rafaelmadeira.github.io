How this test works

1You always have a rack of 7 letters. The first tile can go anywhere. After that, each placement must touch your current connected structure.
2Each uninterrupted horizontal or vertical run of 3+ touching letters is one whole word. You cannot score a valid substring inside a longer invalid run: CSPA is CSPA, not SPA. Two-letter contacts are ignored. Valid words score and disappear; multi-word turns boost Mult and xMult.
3Then the computer places one random letter in one random empty square. Your rack refills to 7 and your next turn begins.
4Shuffle rack is free. Recycle lets you exchange any number of rack letters, but only before placing tiles, and it immediately ends your turn.
5Every 500 points earns a random power. Bomb destroys a board tile, Swap swaps two board tiles, Exchange swaps a rack tile with a board tile, Move relocates one board tile to any empty square, and Wildcard transforms one rack tile into a letter you choose.
6Gold tiles are worth 2×, Diamond tiles are worth 3×, and Frozen tiles thaw the first time they score, then disappear the next time they score.
7Scoring is now Chips × Mult × xMult. Board squares and tile values create Chips. Board Mult grows as the board fills and from special tiles; Play Mult grows from tiles placed, long words, and multi-word plays.
8xMult is the ridiculous layer: 4/5/6/7-letter words contribute ×2/×3/×5/×7, and multi-word turns contribute another ×2/×3/×5. These multiply together.
